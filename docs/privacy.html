Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 August 2026

The short version

Your health record is stored on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in and no analytics, and no server holds your history. When you ask Clairity to explain a document, that one document is sent away to be read by an AI service. That is the only time your health information leaves your device, and it only happens when you ask for it.

What stays on your phone

All of it, by default. Your health profile, saved reports and their explanations, vaccine records, medications, appointments and saved places are written to your device's own storage. Your health card is held in the operating system's encrypted keychain and unlocked with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no copy elsewhere, and no way for anyone to restore it for you. The app offers an export file for exactly this reason.

Each of these is a third party with its own privacy policy, and what they do with what they receive is governed by their terms rather than this one.

Your location

Location is never requested unless you tap the near-me control on a place field, and it is used for one thing: ranking search results so a nearby clinic appears before one in another province. The coordinates are not saved, not written into any field, and not attached to any appointment. Declining the permission leaves everything working, just unranked.

What Clairity does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email address collected.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, tracking pixels or advertising identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of your information. There is nothing to sell, because it is never received.
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking.

Children

Clairity is not directed at children under 13, and their information is not knowingly collected. A parent may of course keep a child's records on their own device.

Not medical advice

Clairity explains what a document says. It does not diagnose, it does not decide what you should do, and it is not a substitute for your doctor, nurse or pharmacist. Explanations are generated by software and can be wrong. Every fact it shows links back to the place on the page it came from so that you can check it. Please do, especially for doses and numbers, and speak to a qualified clinician about anything that matters.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what leaves your device, the date at the top will change and the new version will be published here before the change ships.
