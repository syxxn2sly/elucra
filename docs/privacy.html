Privacy Policy

CLAIRITY · last updated 30 July 2026

The short version. Your health information is stored on your phone, not on our servers — we don't operate any. We have no accounts, no logins and no analytics, so we cannot see your data even if we wanted to. When you ask CLAIRITY to explain a document, that document is sent to an AI service to be read. That is the one time your health information leaves your device, and it only happens when you ask.

What stays on your phone

All of it, by default. Your health profile, saved reports and their explanations, vaccine records, medications, appointments and saved places are written to your device's own storage. Your health card is held in the operating system's encrypted keychain (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) and unlocked with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no copy elsewhere and no way for us to restore it.

What leaves your phone, and when

CLAIRITY has no backend of its own. It talks directly to the services below, only for the feature named, and only when you use that feature.

Each of these is a third party with its own privacy policy, and their handling of what they receive is governed by their terms rather than ours.

Your location

Location is never requested unless you tap the near-me control on a place field, and it is used for one thing: ranking search results so a nearby clinic appears before one in another province. The coordinates are not saved, not written into any field, and not attached to any appointment. Declining the permission leaves everything working, just unranked.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email address collected
  • No analytics, crash reporting, tracking pixels or advertising identifiers
  • No selling or sharing of your information — there is nothing to sell, because we never receive it
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking

Children

CLAIRITY is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. A parent may of course keep a child's records on their own device.

Not medical advice

CLAIRITY explains what a document says. It does not diagnose, does not decide what you should do, and is not a substitute for your doctor, nurse or pharmacist. Explanations are generated by software and can be wrong. Every fact it shows links back to the place on the page it came from so you can check it — please do, especially for doses and numbers, and speak to a qualified clinician about anything that matters.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what leaves your device, the date at the top will change and the new version will be published here before the change ships.
