CLAIRITY

Your medical documents, made clear.

Photograph a lab result, a prescription, a discharge letter — or open the PDF your clinic sent you — and read what it actually says, in plain English.

What it does

Explains the paperwork

What the document says, what the numbers mean next to their normal range, and what you're meant to do next.

Shows you where it got that

Every fact links back to the exact spot on the page it came from, so you can check it against the original rather than take its word.

Reads real paper

Creased, dim, photographed at an angle, handwritten notes in the margin. Also PDFs, which it reads best of all.

Keeps track of the rest

Vaccines and what's due, medications and doses, appointments with reminders, and your health card behind Face ID.

Speaks your language

Explanations can be translated, with the original English kept alongside so nothing is lost in the move.

Stays on your phone

No account, no cloud, no analytics. Your records live on your device and nowhere else.

Careful by design

Medical documents are a bad place to guess. CLAIRITY reads a document in one pass and writes the explanation in another, then checks the explanation back against the page — anything it can't trace to the source is withheld rather than shown, and it tells you when it has withheld something. Where a page was hard to read, it says so instead of quietly presenting a shaky reading as a confident one.

Not medical advice. CLAIRITY explains what your document says. It doesn't diagnose and it isn't a substitute for your doctor, nurse or pharmacist. Explanations are generated by software and can be wrong — check anything that matters, especially doses, with a qualified clinician.
